THE LANTERN VOYAGE + LANTERN HARBOR - Demo 10
=============================================

WHAT'S NEW IN DEMO 10
- Practice clock: "Today X min" sits in the top corner for each child. It counts
  active play only and starts over each day.
- Saves: an old copy of the game left open in another tab can no longer
  overwrite newer progress (this is what reset the parrot).
- Helper's Yard (K5 review) teaches one idea per lesson (18 lessons), with a
  number line on every counting slide. Voices speak slower and pause longer,
  and the videos wait for "Next" between scenes.
- Counting hints and keys use a number line. Adding and taking-away keys use
  the child's own numbers (start at ..., hop ...) without giving the answer.
- Pictures: groups come in fives like a ten-frame; "more/fewer" groups and
  ropes are stacked and lined up at the left; the dozen is a 2 x 6 carton; the
  ordinal ducks face the flag at the front; clearer clock hands; solid figures
  are shaded with hidden edges dashed (square vs triangular pyramid).

WHAT THIS IS
Two courses in one game. Each child profile picks one (Parent area > Children >
Change course; the choice is also on the New Captain screen):
- The Lantern Voyage (2nd-3rd grade): Slice Isle, Kite Cove, Weather Rock and
  Thousand Dunes are playable.
- Lantern Harbor (1st grade): see the LANTERN HARBOR section further down.

Playable islands of The Lantern Voyage:
- Slice Isle: BJU Press Math 2 (5th ed.) Chapter 14, Fractions, lessons 126-135.
- Kite Cove: Chapter 15, Geometry, lessons 136-145. Keeper: Tio Tomas, the kite
  maker. Plane and solid figures, sides and vertices, slide/flip/turn,
  patterns, pattern blocks, Venn diagrams, congruent and similar shapes,
  symmetry, perimeter, and lines. Story: Bezalel (Exodus 31:3). Memory verse:
  Colossians 3:23. Kite Cove opens once Slice Isle's lighthouse is lit (a
  parent preview opens everything).
- Weather Rock: Chapter 16, Metric Measurement, lessons 146-155. Keeper: Dona
  Luz, the weather watcher. Celsius thermometers (to 5 degrees, 10 warmer or
  colder), grams and kilograms, liters, balance scales, centimeters and
  meters, measuring tools, estimating, how much longer, perimeter in cm and
  m, coordinate graphs (ordered pairs), and coding with IF-THEN-ELSE
  (reading programs and fixing a bug, lessons 153-154). Story: Elijah and
  the little cloud (1 Kings 18:44). Memory verse: Isaiah 40:12. Opens once
  Kite Cove's lighthouse is lit.
- Thousand Dunes: Chapter 17, Exploring 4-Digit Numbers, lessons 156-164.
  Keeper: Don Omar, the caravan leader. Counting by hundreds, 10 hundreds =
  1 thousand, place-value blocks and charts, value of a digit, standard and
  expanded form, adding 4-digit numbers (no renaming, renaming ones/tens,
  renaming hundreds), subtracting 4-digit numbers (with and without
  renaming a thousand), and word problems. Story: Abram and the stars
  (Genesis 15:5). Memory verse: Psalm 139:17-18. Opens once Weather Rock's
  lighthouse is lit.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Unzip the folder anywhere on the computer (for example: Documents/Lantern Voyage).
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge (on a Mac: right-click > Open With > Chrome).
   No internet is needed, and nothing needs to be installed.
3. Bookmark the page. Always open it the same way, in the same browser:
   progress is saved inside that browser.

HOW A DAY WORKS
- The island map has one lantern stop per quest. Your child taps a stop and
  presses Start. Every quest ends with a "Lesson complete!" page, then returns
  to the island. Nothing rolls straight into the next quest.
- Daily goal: 3 quests (adjustable). The game stops letting new quests start once
  the daily time limit is reached (40 minutes by default); the current quest
  always finishes.
- First visit to an island: the Lighthouse Check (pretest). Lessons your child
  already knows light up and can be skipped.
- After half the lessons, "Test out early" lets your child take the Lighthouse
  Challenge. Pass it (90%+) and the island is finished. One try per day.

HELP FOR YOUR CHILD
- "Show me how": a picture guide for every skill. New concepts also have a
  "Watch a video" button (needs internet; opens Khan Academy or similar).
- "Walk me through it": an on-screen, step-by-step walkthrough using the
  problem's own numbers. It opens automatically after two misses in practice.
- About every 3rd or 4th multiple-choice problem also asks "How do you know?".
- "Spot the parrot's mistake": the parrot shows an answer and your child checks it.

ARCADE COVE
Five practice games, one token per play, never scored: Pearl Maze (addition),
Lantern Catch (multiplication), Bubble Blaster (subtraction), Lily Pad Hop
(fact families), Sea Serpent (skip counting). Each play picks new numbers.

MASTERY vs. COMPLETION
Finishing a quest lights its lantern. Mastery is tracked separately per skill:
a skill is "mastered" only after 4+ independent right answers on at least two
different days (Parent area > Children > Skill mastery). Help ("Show me how")
is available in lessons and practice, not during scored challenges. The
Lighthouse Challenge needs 90%, with no skill missed twice.

HANDS-ON MISSIONS
Island screen > Hands-on missions. Real-world activities (paper fractions,
baking and sharing, ruler hunt...). Optional; they never block progress. When
your child finishes one, you type the PIN to award the shells.

PARENT AREA (PIN)
- Children: stars, flagged quests, mistake patterns, math facts, recordings.
  "Paper" prints a practice page with an answer key for any lesson.
- Hours & records: time this week, month, quarter, school year, and all time;
  week-by-week table; add off-screen time (workbook pages); CSV download; pace
  tracker with a target finish date.
- Parent Preview: play everything yourself without touching a child's progress.
- Settings: voices, volumes, daily goal, daily time limit, school-year start month.
- Profiles & backup: up to 6 children, archive/restore, export/import.

KEEPING PROGRESS SAFE (IMPORTANT)
Progress lives in the browser. Clearing browsing data ("cookies and site data")
erases progress that is not backed up. The parent area reminds you after 30 days.
- Parent area > Profiles & backup > "Export full backup" (a small .json file).
- Keep the game folder and backups in three places (computer, USB stick, cloud).

VOICES
The cast is pre-recorded and plays offline: narrator, parrot, Abuela Rosa, and
two keeper voices (audio/voice folder). A line without a recording falls back to
the computer's built-in voice. Parent area > Settings can switch voices.
A custom parrot name can't be pre-recorded, so the voice says "your parrot"
for names not on the suggestion list.

The "Tell your parrot" step can optionally record your child's explanation
(the browser asks for microphone permission). Recordings stay on this computer.

LANTERN HARBOR (1ST GRADE)
Your child starts as a regular kid helping Grandpa Eli, the Harbor Master, and
works up the ranks to Captain: Harbor Helper > Dock Hand > Deckhand > Boat
Builder > Navigator-in-Training > First Mate > Captain. Every finished part of
the harbor earns a new piece for the boat they are building, and each rank
changes their outfit. A speckled egg found in Helper's Yard will hatch into
their own parrot later in the year; the parrot then sails with them into The
Lantern Voyage.

Playable now:
- Helper's Yard (K5 readiness): 5 short checks (Find Your Dock, Count the
  Supplies, Load the Cargo, Help the Neighbors, Visit the Village). Each
  covers 3 skill groups with 5 problems each. 80% or more on a group = ready
  (it gets a star and is skipped). Anything lower opens a short teaching
  lesson first. Ends with "The Big Catch" review and memory verse.
  Parent area > Children shows a K5 readiness report.
- Welcome Dock (BJU Math 1, Chapter 1, lessons 1-6): Harbor Check pretest,
  4 lessons, Harbor Review (Psalm 147:4), Chapter Challenge (boat part).
- Seagull Pier (BJU Math 1, Chapter 2, lessons 7-13): numbers 11-20 with
  double ten-frames, a dozen, first to tenth, matching one to one, picture
  and bar graphs, even and odd to 20. Keeper: Fisherman Pete. Story: Jesus
  chooses twelve (Mark 3:13-14). Memory verse: Psalm 90:12. Hands-on:
  gather a dozen eggs, fill two ten-frames, line up 10 ducks, build a bar
  graph, pairs to 20, trace and write 11-20.
- Fish Market (BJU Math 1, Chapter 3, lessons 14-21): addition facts to 6.
  Plus and equals signs, adding zero, addition sentences, story problems
  (draw a picture), adding up and down with dominoes and pennies, names for
  4, 5 and 6, and turn-around facts. Keeper: Senora Marisol. Story: the boy
  who shared five loaves and two fish (John 6:9). Memory verse: Hebrews
  13:16. Hands-on: load ducks in a boat, build addition sentences with
  number and sign cards, sort dominoes by their sum, save pennies, and
  turn facts around.
- The other 18 districts show on the town map as "coming soon".

Built for first graders (a teaching tool first, not a test):
- Every quest starts with a short "Watch & learn" video made in the game
  (works offline): counting animations and sing-along chants over a little
  song. Most also offer a YouTube video from The Singing Walrus, Super Simple
  Songs, NUMBEROCK, Preschool Prep Company or Scratch Garden (needs internet;
  YouTube shows ads and other videos, so watch together).
  The "Show me how" help also has a "Watch the video" button.
- Counting problems: your child taps each object, and the narrator counts out
  loud with each tap. The answer buttons light up after everything is counted.
- Challenges give two tries, hints and a step-by-step walkthrough. Only first
  tries count toward the score.
- Checks (Helper's Yard checks and the Harbor Check) stop early: a topic that
  can no longer pass skips its other questions, and 4 misses ends the check
  and sends your child to the lessons.
- Everything is read aloud, big buttons, no "How do you know?" part, no
  "spot the mistake", 8-problem challenges. No counting by tens in the K5
  review (that comes in BJU Math 1, Chapter 4).
Default day: 2 quests, 20 minutes (Parent area > Settings > Lantern Harbor).
Writing numbers is a hands-on mission plus the printable "Paper" page.

LANTERN HARBOR: DEMO 6 CHANGES
- Slower, smaller steps: each lesson page has one idea; longer pauses between
  sentences; slower counting in videos; every hands-on activity begins with
  "Watch me do the first one" before your child tries.
- A picture "Key" on every question explains the idea (for example, EVEN =
  every one has a partner, ODD = one left over). Tap its speaker to hear it.
- Tracing and writing accept imperfect numbers. Each one is saved in
  Parent area > Children > Handwriting practice (on that device).
- Music is quieter overall, much quieter under voices, and silent during
  hands-on activities and YouTube.
- After the daily goal or time, your child hears "You can be done for today,
  or keep going." Two buttons: I'm done for today / Keep going.
